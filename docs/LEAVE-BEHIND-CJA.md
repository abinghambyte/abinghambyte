# Fewer accidental violations. Lower cost. Defensible records.

**A one-page brief for Larimer County Community Justice Alternatives**

Clearline is a daily check-in service that automatically monitors a client's
drug-test status line, notifies them until they acknowledge it, and gives your
department a proactive dashboard and a court-ready compliance record. It works
with **both** systems you run — the Sentry UA line and the DRC color lines.

---

## The problem, in your numbers

Most program failures aren't dirty tests. In Colorado community corrections,
**~57% of technical discharges are behavioral/programmatic rule violations, not
substance use** (DCJ FY15). A missed daily call-in — a dead phone, a work shift,
a line busy after 5 — becomes a missed test, a violation hearing, and often a
regression. That's a lost per-diem, a jail or prison cost, and a case that failed
for a logistics reason rather than actual drug use.

## What it is

A phone-menu automation + multi-channel notification service. Every day it checks
the client's line and alerts them by **text and voice until acknowledged**. When
it can't confirm a result with high confidence, it tells the client to **call the
line themselves** rather than guess — it never risks a false "you're clear." The
county keeps the data; it can run in your environment if your security team
requires.

## The offer

A **free pilot** with one program and a small group of consenting clients, run
with your team's input and a human reviewing every reading. We measure **one thing
together: the change in accidental missed check-ins** versus the prior comparable
period. You keep the data. No cost, no long-term commitment.
