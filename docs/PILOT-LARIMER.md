# Pilot Target — Larimer County, Colorado

The recommended first pilot. This doc holds the rationale, the entry point, the
outreach draft, and the success metric.

> **Timing note (important, honest):** don't send this until you can actually
> *show* the loop working — even the simulator-driven demo from Phase 1 is
> enough. Pitching "we have a tool" before one exists would misrepresent it and
> burn the relationship. Build the anchor demo first, then reach out.

---

## Why Larimer

- **Runs both line types we support.** 8th Judicial District uses a **Sentry** UA
  line (probation/pretrial) *and* the county Day Reporting Center runs **color
  lines** — a single county that proves both engines at once. (hotline2u dropped
  color code; this is the market gap, live in one place.)
- **County entry point that can move fast.** Community Justice Alternatives (CJA)
  is a *county* department running the DRC and Pretrial Services — far nimbler to
  pilot with than the state judicial branch.
- **Credible co-sign next door.** The 8th District's accredited Adult Drug Court,
  Wellness Court, and nationally-designated DUI Recovery Court are ideal pilot #2
  and grant-application partners.
- **Right-sized volume** (~2,885 new adult cases/yr): enough to show impact,
  small enough to run by hand.
- **Local-founder advantage** if you can meet in person.

## Before you hit send — 3 checks

1. **Incumbent?** Ask whether they already use a notification vendor or the DRC
   has its own app. Position against reality.
2. **Right recipient?** Confirm current CJA leadership (turnover happens).
3. **Partner framing only.** Never auto-dial their lines before there's an
   agreement — the entire strategy is that the agency knows and endorses it.

## Success metric (agree on ONE up front)

**Reduction in accidental missed check-ins** (missed test where the client had no
intent to fail — dead phone, missed call, busy line) over the pilot window,
versus the prior comparable period. Keep the data with the county. This single
number is both the renewal argument and the core asset for a SAMHSA/BJA grant.

## Outreach draft (one page)

> Fill in the bracketed fields. Keep it this short.

**Subject:** A free pilot to cut accidental missed check-ins in Larimer supervision

Dear Ms. Humphrey,

I'm writing about a small, no-cost pilot that could reduce a specific, avoidable
source of violations in your supervision programs: clients who miss a required
test simply because they didn't get that day's call-in result in time.

People on supervision have to call a status line every day. When someone misses
that call — a dead phone, a work shift, a line that's busy after 5 — and their
color or ID was up, it becomes a missed test, and often a violation hearing.
That's a jail bed, staff time, and a setback for someone who wasn't actually
trying to fail.

Our tool checks a client's line automatically every day and notifies them by text
and voice until they acknowledge it. It works with **both** systems Larimer runs
— the Sentry UA line and the Day Reporting Center color lines. When it can't
confirm a result with confidence, it tells the client to call the line themselves
rather than guess. And every check-in is logged into a record the client or an
officer can pull for a hearing.

We'd run a **free pilot** with a small group of your clients, with your team's
knowledge and input, and measure one thing together: the change in accidental
missed check-ins. You keep the data. No cost, no long-term commitment, and we
build around your workflow rather than the other way around.

Could I have 30 minutes to show you how it works and hear whether it fits a
program like the DRC or Pretrial Services? I'm local and glad to come to Midpoint
Drive.

Thank you for the work your team does.

## After a yes

1. Scope the smallest safe pilot (one program, a handful of consenting clients).
2. **Legal/consent** review with the county (recording lines, data handling,
   Colorado two-party-consent).
3. Load their facility scripts (Sentry + the DRC color line) into the engine.
4. Run with a human operator watching every reading; report the metric weekly.
